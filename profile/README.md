Our time is precious — and we should be free to decide what we spend it on.

Having to deal with tasks nobody actually wants steals the time we'd rather spend on what genuinely enriches our lives. So now I grow Kiwis for the boring stuff: built once with full focus, each tool takes one tedious job off our hands to deliver a good result with as little effort as possible.

No faceless corporation — just a small kiwi shed making software that gets out of your way.

## The Canopy

Each Kiwi is built to take on one specific duty:

- 🖥️ **[KiwiDesk](https://kiwidesk.kiwicanopy.com)** — *Tiling that feels like it shipped with macOS.* Stop wasting time dragging windows around: KiwiDesk arranges them for you, opens apps with a shortcut, and remembers your layouts. Mouse or keyboard, your call, and out of your way.
- 📄 **[KiwiCV](https://kiwicv.kiwicanopy.com)** — *Your CV, tailored to every job ad.* A job-specific application in seconds: paste the ad and KiwiCV tailors your CV and drafts the matching cover letter for you to review. Editing and PDF export stay free; AI comes with a Pass.
- 📞 **[KiwiCall](https://kiwicall.kiwicanopy.com)** — *Sudden call? Call back when it suits you.* An Android phone app that reminds you of planned calls, lets you decline a call and plan the callback, and keeps dialing until someone answers.

## Principles

- 🥝 **Built once, with focus:** Each tool tackles one tedious problem and does it well.
- ⚡ **Start simple, grow without limits:** Immediate value out of the box, with depth when you want it.
- 🌿 **Respectful software:** Fast, minimal footprint, and zero nagging.
